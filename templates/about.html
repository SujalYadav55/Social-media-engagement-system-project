How this project works

A short guide to the data, the models and how far you can trust the numbers.

From form to prediction

  1. You describe a post on the Predict page.
  2. Flask turns the form into a row of data: followers, post type, caption length, hashtags, day, hour and call to action.
  3. Two trained models estimate likes per 100 followers and comments per 100 followers.
  4. The app multiplies by your follower count to get likes and comments, and adds the two percentages to get the engagement rate.

MAE is the average size of the miss. R² is 1 for a perfect model and 0 for one no better than guessing the average. Likes and comments score higher than the rate because they mostly follow the follower count, which is easy to predict. The rate is harder because real posts vary a lot for reasons no form can capture.

Limits to keep in mind

  • The data is synthetic, so the patterns the model learned are the ones put into it, not discoveries about real Instagram.
  • The inputs leave out things that matter in real life: image quality, topic, audience, trends and the algorithm.
  • Accounts outside 200 to 500,000 followers fall outside what the model has seen.
